I am an **Associate Professor** and **Undergraduate Program Director** in the Department of Applied Mathematics and Statistics at [SUNY Korea](https://sunykorea.ac.kr), affiliated with [Stony Brook University](https://www.stonybrook.edu/).

### Research Interests

My research lies at the intersection of **applied analysis**, **optimization**, and **control theory**, with particular focus on:

- **Optimal control** of nonsmooth dynamical systems
- **Variational analysis** and **generalized differentiation**
- **Sweeping processes** (Moreau's sweeping process and variants)
- **Differential inclusions** and nonsmooth optimization
- **Reinforcement learning** in nonsmooth dynamical systems

### Education

I received my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from **Wayne State University** (Detroit, MI, USA), where I worked under the supervision of Prof. **Boris Mordukhovich**. Prior to that, I completed my M.S. in Mathematics at the **University of Orléans** (France) and my B.S. in Mathematics at **HCMC University of Pedagogy** (Vietnam).

### Research Grants

- **NRF 기본연구 (2020–2023)** — Principal Investigator, *National Research Foundation of Korea*. Project: *Optimal Control of the Sweeping Process and Its Applications*. Total budget: 165,000,000 KRW.
- **NRF 기본연구B (2026–2030)** — Applied; under review, *National Research Foundation of Korea*.

### Service

I serve as a reviewer for several international journals in optimization, control, and applied mathematics, including *Journal of Optimization Theory and Applications*, *Applied Mathematics & Optimization*, *Set-Valued Analysis*, *Journal of Global Optimization*, *Optimization Letters*, *Applied Mathematical Modelling*, *Evolution Equations and Control Theory*, *Nonlinear Differential Equations and Applications*, *Numerical Algorithms*, and *Acta Mathematica Scientia*.
