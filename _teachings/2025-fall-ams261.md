## Course overview

Multivariable calculus for science and engineering students. Covers differential and integral calculus in $\mathbb{R}^2$ and $\mathbb{R}^3$ with emphasis on vector calculus.

## Main topics

- Vector algebra and geometry in space
- Partial derivatives, gradients, optimization with Lagrange multipliers
- Multiple integrals; change of variables (polar, cylindrical, spherical)
- Vector fields and line integrals
- Surface integrals; Green's, Stokes', and Gauss's theorems

## Prerequisites

AMS 161 (Applied Calculus II) or equivalent.
