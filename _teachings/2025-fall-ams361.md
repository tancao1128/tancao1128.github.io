## Course overview

An introduction to ordinary differential equations and their solution techniques, oriented toward applied and engineering use.

## Main topics

- First-order ODEs: separable, linear, exact equations
- Second-order linear ODEs with constant coefficients; method of undetermined coefficients; variation of parameters
- Linear systems of ODEs; phase-plane analysis
- Laplace transforms and applications
- Power-series solutions; introduction to numerical methods

## Prerequisites

AMS 261 (Applied Calculus III), AMS 210 (Linear Algebra).
