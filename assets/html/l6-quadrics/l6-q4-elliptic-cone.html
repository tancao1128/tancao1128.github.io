Drag inside the figure to rotate. The bold red curve is the trace — the intersection of the surface with the cutting plane.
The same signs as a hyperboloid, but with 0 instead of 1 on the right. It is the boundary case between the one-sheet and two-sheet families: horizontal traces are ellipses that shrink to a single point at the vertex, and the vertical traces through the axis are pairs of lines.
